Strict Mode

The apps that eat your afternoon stay shut.

Pick them once. For as long as the timer runs, iOS Screen Time refuses to open them: not a reminder, not a nudge, a door that doesn't budge. Momo stays open, because that's where you're meant to be.

  • Entirely opt-in. Use it every session, or never.

How it works

One session, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Press start.

    Pick one, five, fifteen or twenty-five minutes, or leave it open-ended. Your buddy settles in to work beside you, and the ring fills as the clock runs.

  2. 02

    Reach for your phone? Shielded.

    With Strict Mode on, the apps you picked won't open until the session ends. The shield goes up and the rabbit holes stay shut.

  3. 03

    Quit, and your buddy feels it.

    Walk away early and your buddy collapses on the spot. Some deflate, some melt into a puddle. No coins for that one, but a tap brings them back.

  4. 04

    Finish, and it celebrates.

    Coins rain onto the ring, your streak grows by a day, and your buddy throws a little party. That is the whole game: show up, stay put, win.

Daily streaks

One session a day keeps the flame lit.

Finish one session and the day counts. String them together and the flame grows, with milestones at a week, a month, a season and a whole year. Miss a day and it starts again. No guilt trips, no begging notifications.
