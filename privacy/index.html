Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version. Momo stores your account, your focus sessions and your progress so the app works, your streaks are kept and the leaderboard means something. Your name, character, level and focus time are visible to other Momo users. There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics SDKs, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising. The apps you pick for Strict Mode never leave your phone. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what Momo: Pomodoro Gamified (the iOS app, "Momo", "the app") and the website at momo-pomodoro.cloud (the "website") collect, why, who processes it, and what you can do about it. Momo is made and run by Yvan Sabay, an individual developer based in the Philippines ("I", "we", "us"). I am the person responsible for your data and you can reach me at sabayyvan2018@gmail.com.

1. What we collect and where it comes from

Account information (from you, or from Apple)

You sign in with either an email address and password or Sign in with Apple. Firebase Authentication (run by Google) stores your email address, a secured form of your password (we never see the password itself), your account ID and sign-in records. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares an email address with us, and with "Hide My Email" that is a private relay address instead of your real one. Apple may also offer your name the first time you sign in, but Momo doesn't store it. When you sign up with email we send a verification email, and we send a reset email if you ask for one.

Your profile (from you, during setup and later)

  • your display name, and a unique @username created from it that can't be changed afterwards;
  • the name you give your buddy (your character), the character you chose, and your equipped pet;
  • your optional answers from onboarding: how you heard about Momo plus any detail you add, roughly how many hours a day you spend on your phone, and your focus goal. These tailor the app, for example the subscription screen repeats your phone-hours answer back to you, and tell us which channels bring people to Momo;
  • your notification settings.

Progress and inventory (generated as you use the app)

Level, XP, weekly XP and weekly focus time, coin balance, streak, the dates of your last completed session and last daily check-in, achievements and when they were unlocked, the characters and pets you own, and summary stats (total and completed sessions, total focus time, longest streak, uninterrupted sessions, multiplayer sessions).

Your focus sessions (generated as you use the app)

The app writes one record each time you try a session, because that is its core function:

  • start and end time, the length you chose, and how long you actually focused;
  • whether it completed or failed, and if it failed, why (you gave up, left the app, or the grace period ran out);
  • how many times it was interrupted;
  • your local date, so streaks and the stats heatmap follow your own timezone;
  • solo or multiplayer, and the game mode;
  • the optional label you can give a session, such as "write report";
  • which character you used, and the XP and coins earned.

Only you can read your session history.

Social features (if you use them)

  • Friends: who you've sent friend requests to or received them from, whether they were accepted, and when. Friend links you share include your account ID so the other person's app can find you.
  • Presence: while you're signed in, a live status (online, focusing or offline), your character's animation state, your character, and the code of a lobby you're in.
  • Co-focus rooms: the room's name, game mode and length, whether it's private, and for each member their display name, character, ready state, animation state, result, the XP and coins they earned and how long they lasted. A private room's password is stored only as a salted hash.
  • Room invites: the inviter's account ID and name, the room name and code, and the time sent.

Subscription and purchase information (from Apple, through RevenueCat)

Purchases are made through your Apple account. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat manages subscriptions for us. It receives your purchase and subscription details from Apple, including product, transaction and trial or renewal status, linked to your Momo account ID so your subscription follows you to a new device. RevenueCat tells our server when your subscription status changes, and we store a simple subscription tier ("pro" or "free") and the time of that change on your profile.

Technical and security data (collected automatically)

  • App Check: to block fake clients and scripts, the app asks Apple's App Attest (or DeviceCheck) to vouch that requests come from a genuine copy of Momo on a real Apple device. Google's Firebase App Check service checks the result. This doesn't identify you.
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can deliver them (see section 6).
  • Server logs: like any online service, our providers keep routine logs, such as request times, IP addresses and error messages, for security and troubleshooting.

2. What other users can see

Momo is social by design. Here is what other signed-in Momo users can see:

  • The global leaderboard (top players by all-time focus time) and the friends leaderboard show your display name, @username, character and pet, level, XP, weekly XP, total and weekly focus time, streak and achievements.
  • Anyone who knows your @username can look you up and send you a friend request.
  • Your presence (online, focusing or offline) is visible to other signed-in users, and friends see it in their friends list.
  • In a co-focus room, other members see your display name, character, ready state and result. Public lobbies list the room name, host's display name, game mode, length and member count to everyone browsing lobbies.
  • If a friend passes you on the weekly leaderboard, accepts your request or invites you to a room, their display name appears in your notification, and yours appears in theirs.

An honest technical note: the app doesn't show other people your coin balance, inventory, subscription tier, check-in dates, notification settings or onboarding answers. However, our current database rules let any signed-in Momo account read a user's profile record, and those fields are stored in it. Someone who modified the app or used developer tools could therefore read them. Please don't put anything private in your names, labels or onboarding answers. Your email address, password, session history, push tokens, Strict Mode choices and invites sent to you are not readable by other users.

3. What stays on your device

  • Strict Mode (iOS Screen Time). When you choose apps to block, Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls frameworks give Momo only an opaque token, not the names or identities of the apps. Momo stores that token on your device to switch the block on and off, and never sends it, or anything about which apps you chose, to us or anyone else. Blocking is carried out by iOS.
  • Local storage. Momo keeps a small store on your device for crash recovery of an active session, your last-used session length, your onboarding answers until you finish setup, and your Strict Mode on/off setting. The Firebase SDK also keeps an offline copy of your own data so the app works without a connection.
  • Widget and Live Activity. The home-screen widget reads a small summary of this week's focus heatmap and your buddy's name stored on your device. The Lock Screen timer (Live Activity) runs on your device.
  • Session reminders. The "session complete", "come back, your session is about to fail" and daily reminder notifications are scheduled locally by your phone, not sent by our server.

Deleting the app removes this local data from your device.

4. What we don't collect

  • No advertising, ad networks, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking. Momo doesn't use App Tracking Transparency because it doesn't track you.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No reading of your other apps, their content, or your Screen Time usage data.
  • No payment card details. Apple handles payment.
  • No uploads of your own files or images.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token with Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers messages through Apple's Push Notification service. The token is stored privately on your account. Our server currently sends push notifications when:

  • a friend invites you to a co-focus room;
  • someone accepts your friend request;
  • a friend passes you on the weekly leaderboard.

You can turn each type, or all of them, off in the app's notification settings, or turn off notifications for Momo in iOS Settings. The token is removed from your account when you sign out or delete your account. We don't send marketing push notifications.

7. The website, the waitlist and reCAPTCHA

Waitlist. If you join the waitlist on momo-pomodoro.cloud, we store your email address, the time you signed up and a note that the sign-up came from the website, in Google Cloud Firestore. We use it only to email you about Momo's launch. We won't sell it or use it for anything else, and you can ask to be removed at any time by emailing sabayyvan2018@gmail.com or by replying to any waitlist email. The waitlist is separate from app accounts, so deleting an app account doesn't remove a waitlist email. Ask us to remove it.

reCAPTCHA. To stop bots abusing the waitlist, the website uses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise through Firebase App Check. It loads only once you start using the waitlist form. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data and interactions with the page, and sends it to Google to assess whether you are a human. This site is protected by reCAPTCHA and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Other website services. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and some scripts from the jsDelivr content network. Like our web host, these services receive your IP address and browser information when your browser requests files from them. The website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Where your data is stored

Momo's servers (Firebase and Google Cloud) are in the United States. RevenueCat and Apple also process data in the United States and other countries. So your data will be transferred to and stored in countries other than your own, which may have different data-protection laws. We choose providers with recognised security and data-protection commitments, and we stay responsible for your data when it's processed abroad, as the Philippines Data Privacy Act requires.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, progress, sessions and friends: for as long as your account exists. We don't currently delete inactive accounts automatically.
  • Presence: overwritten live, and set to offline when you disconnect.
  • Co-focus rooms: temporary. Rooms are removed automatically once they end, empty or go stale, usually within minutes and at most a few hours.
  • Room invites: until the recipient dismisses them.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, delete your account, or Apple reports the token is no longer valid.
  • Waitlist emails: until we've sent launch announcements, and no later than 12 months after the app launches, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period under our providers' standard log retention, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: Apple and RevenueCat keep transaction records for as long as they need to under their own policies and tax and accounting law.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up.

11. Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete account in the app and type DELETE to confirm. This is permanent and immediately deletes:

  • your profile, progress, coins, inventory, achievements and stats;
  • your full session history;
  • your push tokens;
  • every friendship and friend request you're part of, so you disappear from friends' lists;
  • your @username reservation, so it becomes available to others;
  • your presence status and any room invites sent to you;
  • your sign-in account.

What isn't removed immediately:

  • if you were in a co-focus room, your entry stays until the room is cleaned up automatically, usually within hours;
  • room invites you sent to friends stay until they dismiss them;
  • copies in our providers' backup and log systems are removed on their own deletion schedules;
  • purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat, which are kept under their policies and the law. Email us if you'd like us to ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record too;
  • your waitlist email, if you joined on the website. Email us to remove it;
  • local data on your device, until you delete the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or Apple will keep billing you. If you can't use the app, email sabayyvan2018@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google. Database rules stop users reading each other's private data, such as session history and push tokens, and stop anyone except our server from changing your subscription status. App Check rejects requests that don't come from a genuine copy of the app or website. Room passwords are stored only as salted hashes. As a solo developer, I limit access to production data to myself.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee the security of your data. If a breach affects you, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires, including the Philippines National Privacy Commission where applicable.

13. Your rights

Everyone

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • give you a copy of it, in a commonly used electronic format where practical;
  • correct it. You can change your display name, buddy name and notification settings yourself in the app;
  • delete it. You can do this yourself in the app (section 11);
  • stop or limit a use of it, or withdraw consent you've given, such as for notifications or the waitlist.

Email sabayyvan2018@gmail.com. To protect your account, we may ask you to send the request from your account's email address or confirm your identity in another way. We aim to respond within 30 days, and sooner where the law requires. We won't charge you or treat you differently for exercising your rights. There are a few cases where the law lets or requires us to keep data, and if so we'll explain why.

Philippines (Data Privacy Act of 2012, Republic Act No. 10173)

As data subjects, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to rectification, to data portability, to be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of personal data, and to file a complaint. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

United States (including California and other states with privacy laws)

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We don't use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of anyone under 16.
  • The categories we collect are listed in section 1: identifiers (email, account ID, push token), account and profile details, commercial information (subscription status), app activity, and limited technical data. Their sources, purposes and recipients are in sections 1, 5 and 8. We don't collect sensitive personal information as defined by those laws.
  • Depending on your state, you can ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, and to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising (none of which we do). You can use an authorised agent. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and if you're unhappy with the appeal you can contact your state Attorney General.
  • Because we don't sell or share data, Global Privacy Control and "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything we do.

Other countries

If the law where you live gives you further rights, for example to complain to a local data protection authority, you can use them and we'll honour them.

14. Children

Momo isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. You must be at least 13 to use Momo. If you are under 18, or under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email sabayyvan2018@gmail.com and we'll delete the account and its data.

15. Changes to this policy

If Momo changes in a way that affects your data, we'll update this policy and the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. If you don't agree with a change, you can delete your account.
